Frequently Asked Questions

What is a GFCI outlet?

A GFCI (Ground Fault Circuit Interrupter) is an outlet built to protect people from shock. It watches the current going out and coming back, and shuts off power within about 25 milliseconds if the two do not match. Cheap versions sold online are not recommended.

Where are GFCI outlets required?

In the whole kitchen, bathrooms, the garage or carport, any outdoor outlet, unfinished basement areas, the laundry room, and by a garden tub.

How does your quote work?

A licensed electrician visits, looks at the work and gives you a quote for $49.95. The price you are given in writing is what you pay, and you can start right away or come back within 30 days. The quote fee is waived if the work starts right away, or applied to the job when they return.

What does the lifetime warranty cover?

Parts that 50 States Electric supplies. If one fails, the part is always free. Labor to replace it is free in the first year, and after that you pay labor only.

Do you do home inspections for real estate sales?

Yes. Real estate home inspections and electrical safety inspections are both on their service list.

Is it safe to keep using a breaker that keeps tripping?

Not for long. A breaker trips to protect the wiring from overload or a fault. If it keeps happening, turn off what is on that circuit and have it checked.
